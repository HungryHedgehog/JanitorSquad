Janitor Squad is a multiplayer tabletop role playing game (TTRPG)
about a team of agents that go on suicide missions to contain or
destroy dangerous anomalies for a shadowy agency called "The Institute".

Players will slip into the role of the titular "Janitors" an
expendable cleanup crew that gets send in to make first contact
with potential anomalies and try to contain them. Players need to
use their wits, tools and teamwork as consequences of failure may
be deadly.

But death does not mean the end for an employee of the Institute.
Through experimental and anomalous means, new Janitors can be made
from the old, albeit at the risk of significant mutations and the
loss of recent memories.

Janitor Squad takes place in 1980s East-Germany and players must
not only navigate the horrifying anomalies that they are get send
out to clear but also the clandestine nature of cold war
espionage. Other countries have similar institutions and the ever
present risk of getting found out by both western and eastern spy
agencies means Janitors need to act quick and inconspicuous.

This is a beta playtest version.
